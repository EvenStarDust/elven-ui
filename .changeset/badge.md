Add `Badge`: a short label stitched on a small patch with a gilt edge, as a swallowtail `ribbon` (the default), a `shield`, a `round` pill or a `scalloped` patch; in `neutral` (the default), `accent`, `success`, `warning` or `danger`, painted `soft` (a pale tint, the default) or `solid`, at size `sm` or `md`. Adds theme tokens for warnings (`--elven-color-warning`, `--elven-color-on-warning`) and soft status fills (`--elven-color-{neutral,success,warning,danger}-subtle` and `--elven-color-{success,warning,danger}-text`).
