Add `Input`, `DatePicker` and `TimePicker`.

`Input` is a text field on a ruled line (or in a box) with its label, hint and
error built in, and its own controls for password, search and number fields.
`DatePicker` and `TimePicker` replace the browser's unthemeable pickers with a
calendar and a clock that unroll on a scroll of parchment.
